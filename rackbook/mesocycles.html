How to write it

Week 3 Day 1 starts a training day. Type # to search your movements, then the sets: 3x5@7 is 3 sets of 5 at RPE 7, and 3x3@6,7,8 gives one RPE per set. A comma before new sets starts another group: 1x3@8 TS, 3x5@7 BO.

Callouts: TS top set · BO back-off · + AMRAP last set (3x5+) · DS or DS2 drop sets · myo 15-20@9 · DDP · TM90. ME and RE are ignored. The Log page shows callouts as labels; you choose the weights.

Type # to search your movements. Nothing is saved until you load or save the week.
